The 'One Hook at a Time' hmm...

We started with a simple idea: remove ads, enable forwarding. Then things escalated. Now we have so many hooks you could hang a coat on them. Here's what's new in this release:

New Features

Secret Chat Shenanigans

  Disable message deletion in secret chats (yes, really).
  Disable self‑destruct – timers now hit zero and just… stop. Like a movie cliffhanger.
  Save media from secret chats. Your secret is safe with us.

Chat Deletion? What Chat Deletion?

  New option to disable chat deletion entirely. But there's a twist: if you delete a chat while this is on, it will reappear after restart. It's like a chat zombie apocalypse.

Privacy Gains

  Hide your typing indicator – because sometimes you want to lurk in silence.

Improvements

  Settings got a makeover – all hooks are now grouped by category (Ads, Messaging, Secret Chat, Stories, UI, Privacy). No more scrolling through a chaotic list. You're welcome.
  Story saving: now with more saving – we made it work better. 

The Usual Disclaimer

Use at your own risk. We're not responsible if Telegram starts sending you postcards from the Bermuda Triangle or if your self‑destruct timer develops existential dread.
