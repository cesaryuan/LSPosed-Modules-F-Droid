## 🚀 NexAlloy - Official Transition

This release marks the final rebranding of the project and its formal migration to the **NexAlloy Organization**, introducing a new visual identity and a refined foundation.

### ⚠️ Data & Configuration (Action Required)
Since we have switched to new patches, your old YouTube ReVanced settings **will not** carry over automatically. **You must export them before installing this update.**

**Migration Steps:**
1.  **Backup:** Open your current **YouTube** app, go to `Settings -> ReVanced -> Misc -> Import/Export`, and copy your settings.
2.  **Update:** Install NexAlloy over your existing app.
3.  **Restore:** Open **YouTube** again, navigate to the new `Settings -> Morphe -> Misc -> Import/Export`, paste the settings you just copy.
