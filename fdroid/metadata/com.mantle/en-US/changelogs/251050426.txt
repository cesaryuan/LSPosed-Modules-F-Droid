## ⚠️ IMPORTANT: Updater Instructions

**1. Backup before updating!**
Go to `Any Group -> Config Settings -> Export Backup`.

**2. AFTER UPDATE: App Crashing? Quick Fix**
If any app is crashing or force-closing after Mantle is applied, it's likely due to an incompatible device profile. Here's how to fix it in 30 seconds:

**Steps:**
1️⃣ Open **Mantle** → Go to the **Identity** tab
2️⃣ Scroll down to the **Device** section
3️⃣ Tap **CHANGE**
4️⃣ Hit the 🔄 **refresh** icon to reload the latest device list
5️⃣ Tap the **RANDOM** button once — this will assign a new compatible device profile
6️⃣ Reopen the app that was crashing

✅ That's it! The app should work now.

⚠️ **Why does this happen?**
Some device profiles use an older Android version that conflicts with newer apps. Hitting RANDOM picks a fresh profile that's compatible.

If the app still crashes after trying this, repeat the steps once more or send your LSPosed log in DM.
